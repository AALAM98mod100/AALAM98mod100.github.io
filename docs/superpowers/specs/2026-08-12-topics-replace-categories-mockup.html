Deferred, and specified

Browsing by topic from the home page is designed and switched off. Turn it on when one topic passes five posts, on that topic page first; put it under the intro when the home list stops being scannable, around twenty-five posts. It is one line of links in --accent-color, shown here in its home-page state where no topic is active. On a topic page the current one drops to --primary-color and loses its link:

{{ t.name }}{{ t.count }}
Four links here, not five — a topic with no public posts never appears, so Photography joins the line the day its first post ships. Five fit one line at 800px and two lines at 390px. On mobile each link needs display: inline-block and 13px of vertical padding to clear a 44px tap box. Past eight topics, replace the line with a single by topic disclosure that opens the list — a row of links this long is the failure mode.
CSS — NO NEW VARIABLES
Topic links, All topics and All posts use --accent-color, exactly as .post-category does today. Dates and Posted in: use --text-light, descriptions --secondary-color, rules --border-color.
One fix worth making while you are in there: --text-light: #777 is 4.48:1 on white, just under AA. #6f6f6f is 4.9:1 and visually identical. The dark value #8a8a8e already passes.
No cards, shadows, radii, new type sizes or new weights. --shadow is not used anywhere in this design.
Frames above are inline-styled because of the medium; the build stays class-based in components.css and blog.css.
