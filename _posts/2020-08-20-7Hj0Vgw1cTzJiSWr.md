Nala died today. She was around 12 weeks old. I found her on the steps of the masjid in AKBARPURA. Before I found her actually, I had a kind of vision that I didn't tell anyone about.

While praying in my courtyard (Isha I think) I thought I heard a meow. I imagined a grey kitten come from the shadowed alleyway of our house entrance. This was the gate that opened towards the masjid. I just really vividly imagined a kitten. In those times, Saman had asked for a pet cat (only) to be refused by mom and dad. I knew they cannot (would not) refuse a rescue so I yearned for that (circumstance) instead.

Two days alter as I was too later for Maghrib, I saw here on the step. She was tiny, 4-5 weeks old at max. 

As I got closer she started to move away until she reached the edge of the step. She was a kitten after all and her fear of the height overcame her fear of me I guess. I slowly moved my right hand towards her and started scratching her. She looked exactly like the kitten from my imagination. To this day, I don't know what that mean but I took her in right then and there.

She was happy. Annoying but happy. She would have died out there. Is it cruelty to clear your conscience of your treatment to animlas by showing indifference? I think it is. 

"Out of sight, out of mind" for the most of us I guess.

I'm sad. Saman was crying. She must have experienced what I did with Ciri's death only difference being Ciri was much younger and spent much less time when she died. Also, I was not expecting to find Ciri sprawled across like she was the morning. I don't know which one is the more preferable scenario; to see someone/something you love die suddenly and quickly or for them to die a thousand deaths but spend more time with you.

I never wanted to become a doctor for this very reason. I hate sickness. I can't stand it now after my experience with mama's lymphoma and my L4-L5 herniated disc. I just hate the sights and smells of sickness and death too. I don't know if I will ever learn to cope with this in my life. I prefer Ciri died the way she did.

On the other hand, Saman is passionate about being a doctor. She cares about those who are sick. She stood the two nights sleepless with Nala which would turn out to be the last two nights. I think, I'd have to ask sometimes but Saman probably prefers Nala died the way she did, unlike Ciri. I don't know.

I don't know how to cope with grief. I don't know how to cope with loss. I imagined every instance of loss toughening up the soul to withstand more in the future. I don't think this is the case for me atleast. 

I will miss you Nala. You pure, cute, beautiful creature that brought such joy into mine and especially my parents' lives. I know how they will treat their grandchildren because of how they treated you like an absolute baby. Rest in Peace.

From Him we came and to Him we shall return.

In the meantime I'll try my best to be kind to all that dwells on this earth.
